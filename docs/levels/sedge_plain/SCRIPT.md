Words in [square brackets] take the panel's accent colour.

## Who speaks

- **Pell** (`pell`): the ferry child (recurring, shared across levels).
- **Tamsin** (`tamsin`): the mapmaker, camped by the river.
- **Verse-stone** (`verse`): the Ink-maker's words, spoken when a verse-stone is bloomed.
- **(card)** (`card`): a pickup card: no speaker, shown on the paper panel.

### Beat 7

**`verse_2`**: Verse-stone. Trigger: Bloom on the verse-stone on the Knoll (it stands by the sapling; it can be read before or after the fight). Where: `M_Verse_2`. Plays once.

> I boiled this sedge for green when I was young.  
> The green ran out of every pot I filled.  
> So I began to grind a black to keep it in.  
> The plain kept its green without me. I should have asked it how.
